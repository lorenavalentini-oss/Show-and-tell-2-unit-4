🌤️ Weather Adventure

Look · Choose · Say · Color · Play

1. Look2. Weather3. Story4. Week5. Color6. Memory7. Quiz8. Wordwall

🔊 English voice settings

Choose a clear English voice and listen before starting the games. A natural-sounding voice must be available on your device; the page cannot install one.

If the voice sounds robotic, select another voice from the list.

1. Look and talk 🏔️

Look carefully at the picture. Point and say: It's windy! What happens when it's windy?
It's windy! The leaves are flying. The girl's hair is blowing.
What can you see? Select all the things you see.

2. What's the weather like? ☀️

Look at the picture. Listen, then choose the correct weather word. Say the sentence aloud.

3. Read with me: It's Rainy 📖

Look at the five pictures. Tell the story with your teacher. Then choose the correct answers.

It's cloudy and rainy. I'm wet! I'm cold! It's warm in here. Now I'm wet!

4. Weather and days of the week 📅

Look at the weekly forecast from the book. Choose the weather for each day. Say: On Monday, it's windy.

5. Color the weather 🎨

Choose a color. Click or tap a part of the picture to color it. Then say: It's sunny. The sun is yellow. The sky is blue.

I can see a yellow sun!

Teacher tip: Have learners name each color before selecting it.

6. Number Memory Game 🧠

Speaking rule: Before turning a card, say its number in English: "Card number seven, please!" Turn two cards and match a picture with its word. When you find a pair, say: "It's rainy!"

7. Show me! Final quiz ⭐

Look, think, and choose one answer. You will get feedback immediately.
